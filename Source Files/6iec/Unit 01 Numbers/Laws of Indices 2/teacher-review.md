## Student instructions

Simplify each expression fully, evaluating numerical coefficients and combining powers of the same variable. Assume all variables are non-zero. Choose one answer for each question.

## 1. Powers of products and coefficients

Simplify $(3x)^3$ fully.

- A. $9x^3$
- B. $27x^3$
- C. $3x^3$
- D. $27x^1$

Correct answer: B. $27x^3$

Working:

The power applies to every factor inside the brackets, including the coefficient.

$(3x)^3=3^3x^3=27x^3$.

Source: Original.

## 2. Powers of products and coefficients

Simplify $(2x^2)^4$ fully.

- A. $8x^8$
- B. $16x^6$
- C. $16x^8$
- D. $2x^8$

Correct answer: C. $16x^8$

Working:

Raise 2 to the fourth power, then multiply the inner and outer indices on x.

$(2x^2)^4=2^4(x^2)^4=16x^{2\times4}=16x^8$.

Source: Original.

## 3. Multiplying terms and combining laws

Simplify $2x^3\times3x^2$ fully.

- A. $6x^5$
- B. $5x^5$
- C. $6x^6$
- D. $6x^1$

Correct answer: A. $6x^5$

Working:

Multiply the coefficients and add the indices of the common base. Do not add the coefficients.

$2x^3\times3x^2=(2\times3)x^{3+2}=6x^5$.

Source: Original.

## 4. Multiplying terms and combining laws

Simplify $(3a^2)^2\times2a^3$ fully.

- A. $6a^7$
- B. $18a^{12}$
- C. $9a^7$
- D. $18a^7$

Correct answer: D. $18a^7$

Working:

First simplify the bracket: $(3a^2)^2=9a^4$.

Then multiply coefficients and add the remaining indices.

$9a^4\times2a^3=18a^{4+3}=18a^7$.

Source: Original.

## 5. Fractions and combined index laws

Simplify $\frac{(2x^3)^2}{4x^2}$ fully.

- A. $x^8$
- B. $x^4$
- C. $4x^4$
- D. $x^9$

Correct answer: B. $x^4$

Working:

The numerator is $(2x^3)^2=4x^6$.

Divide the coefficients and subtract the denominator's index.

$\frac{4x^6}{4x^2}=\frac44x^{6-2}=x^4$.

Source: Original.

## 6. Fractions and combined index laws

Simplify $\frac{6a^5\times2a^2}{3a^3}$ fully.

- A. $4a^4$
- B. $4a^{10}$
- C. $12a^4$
- D. $4a^1$

Correct answer: A. $4a^4$

Working:

Multiply the numerator's coefficients and add its indices: $6a^5\times2a^2=12a^7$.

Now divide by the denominator and subtract its index.

$\frac{12a^7}{3a^3}=4a^{7-3}=4a^4$.

Source: Original.

## 7. Fractions and combined index laws

Simplify $\frac{(3x^2)^3}{9x^4}$ fully.

- A. $3x^6$
- B. $27x^2$
- C. $3x^{10}$
- D. $3x^2$

Correct answer: D. $3x^2$

Working:

Raise both factors in the numerator to the third power: $(3x^2)^3=27x^6$.

$\frac{27x^6}{9x^4}=3x^{6-4}=3x^2$.

Source: Original.

## 8. Fractions and combined index laws

Simplify $\frac{4a^2\times3a^5}{6a^3\times2a}$ fully.

- A. $2a^3$
- B. $a^4$
- C. $a^3$
- D. $a^7$

Correct answer: C. $a^3$

Working:

Combine the numerator: $4a^2\times3a^5=12a^7$.

Combine the denominator, remembering that $a=a^1$: $6a^3\times2a=12a^4$.

$\frac{12a^7}{12a^4}=a^{7-4}=a^3$.

Source: Original.

## 9. Challenge — all three index laws

Simplify $\frac{(2x^2)^3\times3x}{6x^4}$ fully.

- A. $4x^3$
- B. $4x^7$
- C. $x^3$
- D. $4x^1$

Correct answer: A. $4x^3$

Working:

Apply the power-of-a-product and power-of-a-power rules: $(2x^2)^3=8x^6$.

Multiply the numerator: $8x^6\times3x=24x^7$.

Finally divide the coefficients and subtract the indices.

$\frac{24x^7}{6x^4}=4x^{7-4}=4x^3$.

Source: Original.

## 10. Challenge — all three index laws

Simplify $\frac{(3a^2)^2\times(2a)^3}{6a^5}$ fully.

- A. $6a^2$
- B. $12a^{12}$
- C. $12a^2$
- D. $72a^2$

Correct answer: C. $12a^2$

Working:

Simplify both bracketed powers: $(3a^2)^2=9a^4$ and $(2a)^3=8a^3$.

Multiply the numerator: $9a^4\times8a^3=72a^7$.

Divide coefficients and subtract the denominator's index.

$\frac{72a^7}{6a^5}=12a^{7-5}=12a^2$.

Source: Original.
