Choose one answer for each question. Give answers as fractions in their simplest form; an improper fraction is allowed.

## 1. Adding and subtracting fractions

Calculate 2/9+4/9. Give your answer as a fraction in its simplest form.

- A. 2/3
- B. 1/3
- C. 4/9
- D. 1/2

Correct answer: A. 2/3

Working: Use a common denominator of 9.  2/9+4/9=2/9+4/9=6/9.  Simplify: 6/9=2/3.

## 2. Adding and subtracting fractions

Calculate 7/10-3/10. Give your answer as a fraction in its simplest form.

- A. 1/5
- B. 2/5
- C. 3/5
- D. 1/2

Correct answer: B. 2/5

Working: Use a common denominator of 10.  7/10-3/10=7/10-3/10=4/10.  Simplify: 4/10=2/5.
