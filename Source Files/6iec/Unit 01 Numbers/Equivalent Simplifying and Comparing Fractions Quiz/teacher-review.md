## 1. Equivalent fractions

Find the missing numerator: 2/3=(□)/12.

- A. 6
- B. 8
- C. 9
- D. 10

Correct answer: B. 8

Working: The denominator is multiplied by 4, so multiply the numerator by 4 as well.  2×4=8, so 2/3=8/12.

## 2. Equivalent fractions

Which fraction is equivalent to 3/5?

- A. 6/15
- B. 9/10
- C. 12/25
- D. 9/15

Correct answer: D. 9/15

Working: Multiply both numerator and denominator by 3.  (3×3)/(5×3)=9/15.

## 3. Simplifying fractions

Write 12/18 in its simplest form.

- A. 3/4
- B. 1/3
- C. 2/3
- D. 4/9

Correct answer: C. 2/3

Working: Divide the numerator and denominator by their highest common factor, 6.  12/18=(12÷ 6)/(18÷ 6)=2/3.

## 7. Ordering and comparing fractions

Choose the symbol that makes this statement true: 3/4 □ 5/8.

- A. <
- B. =
- C. >
- D. They cannot be compared.

Correct answer: C. >

Working: Write both fractions with denominator 8.  3/4=6/8,  5/8=5/8.  Compare the numerators: 3/4>5/8.

## 9. Ordering and comparing fractions

Put these fractions in order from smallest to largest: 3/4, 1/2, 2/3.

- A. 3/4, 2/3, 1/2
- B. 1/2, 2/3, 3/4
- C. 2/3, 1/2, 3/4
- D. 1/2, 3/4, 2/3

Correct answer: B. 1/2, 2/3, 3/4

Working: Use a common denominator of 12:  3/4=9/12,  1/2=6/12,  2/3=8/12.  From smallest to largest: 1/2, 2/3, 3/4.

## 10. Ordering and comparing fractions

Put these fractions in order from smallest to largest: 7/10, 3/5, 2/3.

- A. 3/5, 7/10, 2/3
- B. 2/3, 3/5, 7/10
- C. 3/5, 2/3, 7/10
- D. 7/10, 2/3, 3/5

Correct answer: C. 3/5, 2/3, 7/10

Working: Use a common denominator of 30:  7/10=21/30,  3/5=18/30,  2/3=20/30.  From smallest to largest: 3/5, 2/3, 7/10.
