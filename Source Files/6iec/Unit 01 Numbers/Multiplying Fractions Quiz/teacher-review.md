## Student instructions

Multiply each pair of fractions and give your answer in its simplest form. Simplify the given fractions or cancel common factors before multiplying where helpful. No mixed numbers are used.

## 1. Multiplying and simplifying

Calculate $\frac{2}{5}\times\frac{3}{7}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{5}{12}$
- B. $\frac{6}{35}$
- C. $\frac{6}{12}$
- D. $\frac{1}{7}$

Correct answer: B. $\frac{6}{35}$

Worked solution:

Multiply the numerators together and the denominators together.

$\frac25\times\frac37=\frac{2\times3}{5\times7}=\frac6{35}$.

6 and 35 have no common factor greater than 1, so the answer is already in its simplest form.

Source: Original.

## 2. Multiplying and simplifying

Calculate $\frac{3}{4}\times\frac{2}{5}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{5}{9}$
- B. $\frac{3}{20}$
- C. $\frac{3}{10}$
- D. $\frac{6}{9}$

Correct answer: C. $\frac{3}{10}$

Worked solution:

Multiply, then simplify by dividing numerator and denominator by 2.

$\frac34\times\frac25=\frac6{20}=\frac3{10}$.

Source: Original.

## 3. Multiplying and simplifying

Calculate $\frac{4}{9}\times\frac{3}{8}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{1}{6}$
- B. $\frac{7}{17}$
- C. $\frac{1}{18}$
- D. $\frac{1}{3}$

Correct answer: A. $\frac{1}{6}$

Worked solution:

Cancel 4 with 8 (divide both by 4), and 3 with 9 (divide both by 3).

$\frac49\times\frac38=\frac13\times\frac12=\frac16$.

## 4. Simplify first and cancel common factors

Calculate $\frac{6}{15}\times\frac{5}{8}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{1}{8}$
- B. $\frac{11}{23}$
- C. $\frac{1}{2}$
- D. $\frac{1}{4}$

Correct answer: D. $\frac{1}{4}$

Worked solution:

First simplify $\frac6{15}=\frac25$.

Then cancel the common factor 5 across the product.

$\frac25\times\frac58=\frac28=\frac14$.

Source: Original.

## 5. Simplify first and cancel common factors

Calculate $\frac{7}{12}\times\frac{18}{35}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{3}{5}$
- B. $\frac{3}{10}$
- C. $\frac{1}{10}$
- D. $\frac{5}{14}$

Correct answer: B. $\frac{3}{10}$

Worked solution:

Cancel 7 with 35 (divide both by 7), and 18 with 12 (divide both by 6).

$\frac7{12}\times\frac{18}{35}=\frac12\times\frac35=\frac3{10}$.

Source: Original.

## 6. Simplify first and cancel common factors

Calculate $\frac{13}{80}\times\frac{20}{39}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{1}{12}$
- B. $\frac{1}{6}$
- C. $\frac{1}{4}$
- D. $\frac{1}{39}$

Correct answer: A. $\frac{1}{12}$

Worked solution:

Cancel 13 with 39 (divide both by 13), and 20 with 80 (divide both by 20).

$\frac{13}{80}\times\frac{20}{39}=\frac14\times\frac13=\frac1{12}$.

This avoids multiplying 13 by 20 and 80 by 39 before reducing.

Source: Original.

## 7. Simplify first and cancel common factors

Calculate $\frac{9}{14}\times\frac{21}{27}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{1}{3}$
- B. $\frac{3}{4}$
- C. $\frac{1}{6}$
- D. $\frac{1}{2}$

Correct answer: D. $\frac{1}{2}$

Worked solution:

First simplify $\frac{21}{27}=\frac79$. Then cancel the common factor 9.

$\frac9{14}\times\frac79=\frac7{14}=\frac12$.

Source: Original.

## 8. Cancellation challenge

Calculate $\frac{14}{45}\times\frac{15}{28}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{1}{3}$
- B. $\frac{1}{12}$
- C. $\frac{1}{6}$
- D. $\frac{2}{9}$

Correct answer: C. $\frac{1}{6}$

Worked solution:

Cancel 14 with 28 (divide both by 14), and 15 with 45 (divide both by 15).

$\frac{14}{45}\times\frac{15}{28}=\frac13\times\frac12=\frac16$.

Source: Original.

## 9. Cancellation challenge

Calculate $\frac{25}{42}\times\frac{14}{75}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{1}{9}$
- B. $\frac{1}{3}$
- C. $\frac{2}{9}$
- D. $\frac{1}{15}$

Correct answer: A. $\frac{1}{9}$

Worked solution:

Cancel 25 with 75 (divide both by 25), and 14 with 42 (divide both by 14).

$\frac{25}{42}\times\frac{14}{75}=\frac13\times\frac13=\frac19$.

## 10. Cancellation challenge

Calculate $\frac{16}{33}\times\frac{22}{40}$. Give your answer in its simplest form.

- A. $\frac{2}{15}$
- B. $\frac{4}{5}$
- C. $\frac{4}{15}$
- D. $\frac{2}{3}$

Correct answer: C. $\frac{4}{15}$

Worked solution:

First simplify $\frac{22}{40}=\frac{11}{20}$.

Cancel 11 with 33 (divide both by 11), and 16 with 20 (divide both by 4).

$\frac{16}{33}\times\frac{11}{20}=\frac43\times\frac15=\frac4{15}$.

Source: Original.
