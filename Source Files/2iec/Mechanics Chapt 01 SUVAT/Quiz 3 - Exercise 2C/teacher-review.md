## 2C Q1

A ball is projected vertically upwards from O at $14\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find its greatest height above O.

- A. $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- B. $1.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- C. $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- D. $10\,\mathrm{m}$

Correct answer: D. $10\,\mathrm{m}$

Worked solution:

Take upwards as positive, so $a=-9.8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. At the highest point $v=0$.

From $v^2=u^2+2as$, $0=14^2-19.6s$.

$$s=\frac{196}{19.6}=10\,\mathrm{m}.$$

## 2C Q2

A well is $50\,\mathrm{m}$ deep. A stone is released from rest at the top. Find the time it takes to reach the bottom.

- A. $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- B. $3.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- C. $2.3\,\mathrm{s}$
- D. $5.1\,\mathrm{s}$

Correct answer: B. $3.2\,\mathrm{s}$

Worked solution:

Take downwards as positive, with $u=0$, $a=9.8$ and $s=50$.

$$50=\frac12(9.8)t^2=4.9t^2.$$

The positive root is $t=\sqrt{50/4.9}=3.19438\ldots$, so $t=3.2\,\mathrm{s}$ (2 s.f.).

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 2; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 23; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 38 (Exercise C, Question 2).

## 2C Q4

A particle is projected vertically upwards from the ground at $20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find its time of flight until it returns to the ground.

- A. $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- B. $8.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- C. $4.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- D. $0\,\mathrm{s}$

Correct answer: C. $4.1\,\mathrm{s}$

Worked solution:

Take upwards as positive. At the return to the ground, $s=0$:

$$0=20t-4.9t^2=t(20-4.9t).$$

Reject $t=0$, the launch instant. The flight time is $20/4.9=4.08163\ldots\approx4.1\,\mathrm{s}$ (2 s.f.), not just the time to the highest point.

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 4; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 23; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 40 (Exercise C, Question 4).

## 2C Q6

A pebble is catapulted vertically upwards at $24\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find (a) its greatest height above the point of projection and (b) the time taken to reach that height.

- A. a) $29\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $2.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- B. a) $29\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $4.9\,\mathrm{s}$
- C. a) $59\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $2.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- D. a) $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $29\,\mathrm{s}$

Correct answer: A. a) $29\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $2.4\,\mathrm{s}$

Worked solution:

Take upwards as positive; at the highest point $v=0$.

(a) $0=24^2-19.6h$, giving $h=29.3878\ldots\approx29\,\mathrm{m}$ (2 s.f.).

(b) $0=24-9.8t$, so $t=24/9.8=2.44898\ldots\approx2.4\,\mathrm{s}$ (2 s.f.).

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 6; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 23; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 42 (Exercise C, Question 6).

## 2C Q8

P is projected vertically downwards from $80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground at $4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find (a) its speed on impact and (b) its time to reach the ground.

- A. a) $40\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $4.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- B. a) $36\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $3.7\,\mathrm{s}$
- C. a) $16\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- D. a) $40\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $3.7\,\mathrm{s}$

Correct answer: D. a) $40\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $3.7\,\mathrm{s}$

Worked solution:

Take downwards as positive: $u=4$, $a=9.8$, $s=80$.

(a) $v^2=4^2+2(9.8)(80)=1584$, so $v=\sqrt{1584}=39.7995\ldots\approx40\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ (2 s.f.).

(b) Use the unrounded impact speed:

$$t=\frac{\sqrt{1584}-4}{9.8}.$$

Thus $t=3.65301\ldots\approx3.7\,\mathrm{s}$ (2 s.f.).

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 8; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 23; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 44 (Exercise C, Question 8).

## 2C Q10

A ball is thrown vertically upwards at $21\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and hits the ground $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$ later. Find the height above the ground from which it was thrown.

- A. $-4.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- B. $94\,\mathrm{m}$
- C. $4.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- D. $190\,\mathrm{m}$

Correct answer: C. $4.7\,\mathrm{m}$

Worked solution:

Take upwards as positive. After $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$,

$$s=21(4.5)-4.9(4.5)^2=-4.725\,\mathrm{m}.$$

The ground is below the launch point. The requested height is the positive magnitude $4.725\approx4.7\,\mathrm{m}$ (2 s.f.), not the signed displacement.

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 10; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 23; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 46 (Exercise C, Question 10).

## 2C Q11

A stone is thrown vertically upwards at $16\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ from $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. Find (a) its time of flight and (b) the total distance it travels before hitting the ground.

- A. a) $3.4\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- B. a) $3.4\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $29\,\mathrm{m}$
- C. a) $1.6\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- D. a) $3.4\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $16\,\mathrm{m}$

Correct answer: B. a) $3.4\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $29\,\mathrm{m}$

Worked solution:

Take upwards as positive. The final displacement from launch is $-3\,\mathrm{m}$.

(a) $-3=16t-4.9t^2$, so $4.9t^2-16t-3=0$.

$$t=\frac{16+\sqrt{314.8}}{9.8}.$$

The negative root is not an elapsed flight time. The positive root gives $t\approx3.4\,\mathrm{s}$ (2 s.f.).

(b) The rise above launch is $h=\frac{16^2}{19.6}=13.0612\ldots\,\mathrm{m}$. The descent is $h+3$.

Total distance $=2h+3=29.1224\ldots\approx29\,\mathrm{m}$ (2 s.f.). Keep full precision until the final rounding.

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 11; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 23; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 47 (Exercise C, Question 11).

## 2C Q12

A particle is projected vertically upwards at $24.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find the total time for which it is $21\,\mathrm{m}$ or more above its point of projection.

- A. $2.8\,\mathrm{s}$
- B. $1.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- C. $3.9\,\mathrm{s}$
- D. $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$

Correct answer: A. $2.8\,\mathrm{s}$

Worked solution:

Take upwards as positive. The two crossings of height $21\,\mathrm{m}$ satisfy $21=24.5t-4.9t^2$.

$$4.9t^2-24.5t+21=0.$$

The quadratic formula uses denominator $2(4.9)=9.8$:

$$t=\frac{24.5\pm\sqrt{188.65}}{9.8}.$$

The crossing times are $1.09847\ldots$ and $3.90153\ldots\,\mathrm{s}$. Between them the height is at least $21\,\mathrm{m}$.

Their difference is $2.80306\ldots\approx2.8\,\mathrm{s}$ (2 s.f.).

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 12; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 23; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 48 (Exercise C, Question 12).

## 2C Q15

A ball is released from rest $10\,\mathrm{m}$ above a wooden floor. Each impact is followed by a rebound at three-quarters of the impact speed. Find its greatest height above the floor after (a) the first rebound and (b) the second rebound.

- A. a) $7.5\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $5.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- B. a) $5.6\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $4.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- C. a) $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $0.63\,\mathrm{m}$
- D. a) $5.6\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $3.2\,\mathrm{m}$

Correct answer: D. a) $5.6\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $3.2\,\mathrm{m}$

Worked solution:

The first impact speed is $\sqrt{2(9.8)(10)}=14\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

(a) The first rebound speed is $\frac34(14)=10.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

$$h_1=\frac{10.5^2}{19.6}=5.625\,\mathrm{m}.$$

Thus $h_1\approx5.6\,\mathrm{m}$ (2 s.f.).

(b) The second impact speed is again $10.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, and the second rebound speed is $\frac34(10.5)=7.875\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

$$h_2=\frac{7.875^2}{19.6}=3.1640625\,\mathrm{m}.$$

So $h_2\approx3.2\,\mathrm{m}$ (2 s.f.). The height ratio per rebound is $(\frac34)^2$, not $\frac34$.

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 15; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 24; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF pages 51–52 (Exercise C, Question 15).

## 2C Q16

P is projected vertically upwards from O at $12\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. One second later Q is projected vertically upwards from O at $20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find (a) the time from P's launch until they collide and (b) the height of the collision above O.

- A. a) $0.40\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- B. a) $1.4\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- C. a) $1.4\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- D. a) $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$

Correct answer: B. a) $1.4\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$

Worked solution:

Take upwards as positive and let $t$ be the time since P's launch. Q has been in flight for $t-1$ seconds.

$$s_P=12t-4.9t^2.$$

$$s_Q=20(t-1)-4.9(t-1)^2.$$

(a) Equating displacements gives $24.9=17.8t$, hence $t=\frac{24.9}{17.8}=1.39888\ldots\approx1.4\,\mathrm{s}$ (2 s.f.). This is measured from P's launch, not Q's.

(b) Substitute the unrounded time into $s_P=12t-4.9t^2$. This gives $s_P\approx7.2\,\mathrm{m}$ (2 s.f.).

Source: M1, Exercise 2C, Question 16; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 24; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 53 (Exercise C, Question 16).
