## 2B Q1

A particle moves in a straight line with constant acceleration $2.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. It passes A at $3\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and later B, where $AB=8$ m. Find its speed at B.

- A. $49\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- B. $5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- C. $7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- D. $23\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Correct answer: C. $7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Worked solution:

Use $v^2=u^2+2as$.

$$v^2=3^2+2(2.5)(8)=49.$$

Speed is non-negative: $v=\sqrt{49}=7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## 2B Q3

A cyclist travelling at $12\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ brakes and comes to rest after moving $36\,\mathrm{m}$ in a straight line. Assuming uniform deceleration, find the deceleration.

- A. $2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$
- B. $-2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$
- C. $4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$
- D. $0.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$

Correct answer: A. $2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$

Worked solution:

Take the initial direction as positive and use $v^2=u^2+2as$.

$$0=12^2+2a(36),\qquad a=-2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}.$$

The deceleration is the positive magnitude $2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 3; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 16; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 22 (Exercise B, Question 3).

## 2B Q5

A particle moves from O towards A along a straight line with constant acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. Its speed at O is $5.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $OA=20$ m. Find the time taken from O to A.

- A. $-8\,\mathrm{s}$
- B. $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- C. $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- D. $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$

Correct answer: D. $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$

Worked solution:

Use $s=ut+\frac12at^2$: $20=5.5t+t^2$.

$$2t^2+11t-40=(2t-5)(t+8)=0.$$

The roots are $t=2.5$ and $t=-8$. Reject the negative elapsed time, leaving $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$.

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 5; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 16; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 24 (Exercise B, Question 5).

## 2B Q6

A train accelerates uniformly along a straight horizontal track. It passes two signals at $54\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}$ and $72\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}$; they are $500\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Find its acceleration in m/s².

- A. $0.018\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$
- B. $0.175\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$
- C. $0.35\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$
- D. $2.268\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$

Correct answer: B. $0.175\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$

Worked solution:

Convert to SI units: $54\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}=15\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $72\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}=20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

Use $v^2=u^2+2as$:

$$a=\frac{20^2-15^2}{2(500)}=0.175\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}.$$

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 6; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 16; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 25 (Exercise B, Question 6).

## 2B Q8

A particle moves $38\,\mathrm{m}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$ along a straight line with constant acceleration $3\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. Find (a) its initial speed and (b) its final speed.

- A. a) $9.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $21.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- B. a) $3.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $12\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- C. a) $3.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $15.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- D. a) $15.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $3.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Correct answer: C. a) $3.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; b) $15.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Worked solution:

(a) $s=ut+\frac12at^2$ gives $38=4u+\frac12(3)(4^2)=4u+24$. Thus $u=3.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

(b) $v=u+at=3.5+3(4)=15.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 8; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 16; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 27 (Exercise B, Question 8).

## 2B Q10

A stone slides in a straight line across a frozen lake, initially at $12\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Friction causes constant deceleration $0.8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. Find (a) the distance before it stops and (b) its speed after travelling half this distance. Give (b) to 3 significant figures.

- A. a) $90\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $6\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- B. a) $45\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $8.49\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- C. a) $180\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $8.49\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- D. a) $90\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $8.49\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Correct answer: D. a) $90\,\mathrm{m}$; b) $8.49\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Worked solution:

(a) With $a=-0.8$, $0=12^2-1.6s$, so $s=90\,\mathrm{m}$.

(b) Half the distance is $45\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$v^2=12^2+2(-0.8)(45)=72.$$

Hence the speed is $\sqrt{72}=8.48528\ldots\approx8.49\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ (3 s.f.). It is not half the initial speed.

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 10; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 16; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 29 (Exercise B, Question 10).

## 2B Q12

A particle moves horizontally with constant acceleration $-2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, taking AB as positive. At $t=0$ it passes A at $12\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; $AB=32$ m. The acceleration remains constant after it reverses direction. Find (a) both times it is at B and (b) the corresponding signed velocities, in the same order.

- A. a) $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and $8\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $+4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $-4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- B. a) $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and $8\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $+4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $+4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
- C. a) $4\,\mathrm{s}$ only; b) $+4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ only
- D. a) $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $8\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $+8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $-4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Correct answer: A. a) $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and $8\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $+4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $-4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Worked solution:

(a) Use $s=ut+\frac12at^2$: $32=12t-t^2$.

$$(t-4)(t-8)=0,\qquad t=4\,\mathrm{s}\text{ or }8\,\mathrm{s}.$$

(b) $v=12-2t$, so the velocities are $+4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ at $t=4$ and $-4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ at $t=8$. The second crossing is on the return journey.

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 12; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 16; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 31 (Exercise B, Question 12).

## 2B Q14

A particle P passes the origin at $t=0$ with velocity $+14\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and constant acceleration $-4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. Point A has coordinate $x=22.5$ m. Find (a) the difference between the two times P passes A and (b) the total distance travelled between these crossings.

- A. a) $2\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- B. a) $2\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- C. a) $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- D. a) $2\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $2\,\mathrm{m}$

Correct answer: B. a) $2\,\mathrm{s}$; b) $4\,\mathrm{m}$

Worked solution:

(a) $22.5=14t-2t^2$, so

$$(2t-5)(2t-9)=0.$$

The crossing times are $2.5$ and $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$, a difference of $2\,\mathrm{s}$.

(b) The turning time is $14/4=3.5\,\mathrm{s}$. Its coordinate then is $14(3.5)-2(3.5)^2=24.5\,\mathrm{m}$.

The distance out and back is $2(24.5-22.5)=4\,\mathrm{m}$, although net displacement is zero.

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 14; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 17; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 33 (Exercise B, Question 14).

## 2B Q15

A car moves forwards along a straight horizontal road with constant acceleration, passing consecutive points A, B and C. $AB=100$ m, $BC=300$ m, and its speeds at B and C are $14\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find (a) its acceleration and (b) the time from A to C, giving (b) to 3 significant figures.

- A. a) $0.34\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$; b) $17.6\,\mathrm{s}$
- B. a) $0.68\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$; b) $25.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- C. a) $0.34\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$; b) $25.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- D. a) $0.255\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$; b) $25.5\,\mathrm{s}$

Correct answer: C. a) $0.34\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$; b) $25.5\,\mathrm{s}$

Worked solution:

(a) On BC, $20^2=14^2+2a(300)$, giving $a=0.34\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.

(b) The distance AC is $400\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence $20^2=u_A^2+2(0.34)(400)$, so $u_A^2=128$.

The car is moving forwards, so $u_A=\sqrt{128}=8\sqrt2$, not the negative square root.

$$t=\frac{20-8\sqrt2}{0.34}.$$

This is $25.5479\ldots\,\mathrm{s}$, so $t=25.5\,\mathrm{s}$ (3 s.f.). Keep unrounded values until the final step.

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 15; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 17; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF page 34 (Exercise B, Question 15).

## 2B Q16

P and Q move along the same straight horizontal line with constant accelerations $2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ and $3.6\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. P passes A at $t=0$ at $4\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. One second later Q passes A at $3\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, in the same direction. For $t\geq1$, find (a) their displacements from A as functions of t, (b) when they meet, measured from P passing A, and (c) the distance of the meeting point from A.

- A. a) $s_P=4t+t^2$ and $s_Q=3t+1.8t^2$ (in metres); b) $6\,\mathrm{s}$; c) $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- B. a) $s_P=4t+t^2$ and $s_Q=3(t-1)+1.8(t-1)^2$ (in metres); b) $5\,\mathrm{s}$; c) $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- C. a) $s_P=4t+t^2$ and $s_Q=3(t-1)+1.8(t-1)^2$ (in metres); b) $6\,\mathrm{s}$; c) $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- D. a) $s_P=4t+t^2$ and $s_Q=3(t-1)+1.8(t-1)^2$ (in metres); b) $6\,\mathrm{s}$; c) $60\,\mathrm{m}$

Correct answer: D. a) $s_P=4t+t^2$ and $s_Q=3(t-1)+1.8(t-1)^2$ (in metres); b) $6\,\mathrm{s}$; c) $60\,\mathrm{m}$

Worked solution:

(a) Measure displacement in the common forward direction. P has travelled for $t$ seconds; Q has travelled for $t-1$ seconds since passing A.

$$s_P=4t+t^2.$$

$$s_Q=3(t-1)+1.8(t-1)^2.$$

(b) Set $s_P=s_Q$ and simplify: $0.8t^2-4.6t-1.2=0$.

$$4t^2-23t-6=(t-6)(4t+1)=0.$$

Reject $t=-\frac14$; the applicable meeting time is $6\,\mathrm{s}$, measured from P passing A (5 s after Q passes A).

(c) $s_P=4(6)+6^2=60\,\mathrm{m}$.

Source: M1, Exercise 2B, Question 16; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/M1 chapter 1 exercises.pdf, PDF page 17; Source Files/2iec/Mechanics Chapt 01 SUVAT/Solutions chapter 01.pdf, PDF pages 35–36 (Exercise B, Question 16).
