C4 Partial Fractions Quiz

Netlify deployment:
1. Open Netlify.
2. Choose "Add new site" > "Deploy manually".
3. Drag the partial-fractions-quiz folder into Netlify, or upload the zip file.
4. Netlify will publish index.html as the quiz page.

The quiz contains Worksheet A questions 1, 2 and 3, with 4 clickable multiple-choice answers per item. Students see their score only after submitting the full quiz.
